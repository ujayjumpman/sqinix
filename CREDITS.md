# Credits and licences

## Imagery and video
All hardware imagery and the hero loop are **procedurally generated by this project** (`scripts/render/`, built with three.js). No third-party photographs, footage or 3D models are used. The generic hardware shapes carry no real brands or logos.

## Logo
Traced from the logo PNG published on the current squinix.com (`scripts/trace-logo.mjs`). Squinix owns its brand; replace with the original vector artwork when available.
